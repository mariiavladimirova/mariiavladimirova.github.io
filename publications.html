  • Fairness Failure in Generative Models is an Evaluation Problem
    Mariia Vladimirova, Jean-Yves Franceschi, Thibaut Issenhuth
    ICMLPosition Paper2026
    Project page HAL Code & data
    This position paper argues that fairness failures in generative models are ultimately an evaluation problem: fairness findings are rarely comparable across papers or actionable for deployment decisions. We diagnose recurring empirical and conceptual failure modes and propose Fairness Cards as a minimal reporting artifact that makes evaluation choices explicit (covering prompt protocols, decoding settings, refusal handling, and scorer details), enabling reproducibility, comparability, and accountability.

  • Fairness Through Independence via Cramér-von Mises Regularization
    Albert Gimó Contreras, Mariia Vladimirova, Olga Petrova, Federico Pavone, Reda Chhaibi
    NeurIPSWorkshopReliable ML Workshop, 2025
    We introduce a CvM-based regularizer that promotes statistical independence between model outputs and sensitive attributes, with an efficient differentiable sample-based implementation. We provide theoretical analysis connecting CvM to demographic parity and equalized odds, and demonstrate competitive utility-fairness trade-offs on tabular benchmarks.
    @inproceedings{contreras2025cvm, title={Fairness Through Independence via {Cram\'{e}r-von Mises} Regularization}, author={Contreras, Albert Gim{\'o} and Vladimirova, Mariia and Petrova, Olga and Pavone, Federico and Chhaibi, Reda}, booktitle={NeurIPS Workshop on Reliable Machine Learning}, year={2025} }
  • CUVET: A Partitioning Approach for Continuous Treatment Assignment At Scale
    Artem Betlei, Mariia Vladimirova, Victor Girou, Thibaud Rahier
    NeurIPSWorkshopNeurIPS CauScien Workshop, 2025
    Dataset
    CUVET (Cost per Unit Value Equalization Tree) is a novel method for continuous treatment assignment that recursively partitions the user space to equalize marginal cost per unit value across cohorts. The method yields a closed-form cost-aware policy and scales to large digital advertising datasets. We also introduce the CUVET-policy benchmark, an open-source dataset derived from real-world A/B experiments.
    @inproceedings{betlei2025cuvet, title={{CUVET}: A Partitioning Approach for Continuous Treatment Assignment At Scale}, author={Betlei, Artem and Vladimirova, Mariia and Girou, Victor and Rahier, Thibaud}, booktitle={NeurIPS Workshop on Uncovering Causality in Science}, year={2025} }

  • FairJob: A Real-World Dataset for Fairness in Online Systems
    Mariia Vladimirova, Eustache Diemert, Federico Pavone
    NeurIPSDatasets & Benchmarks, 2024
    PDF Dataset Blog post
    We present FairJob, a real-world dataset for studying fairness in job recommendation within online advertising. A key contribution is a method for obtaining proxy estimates of protected attributes not directly observable, while preserving a realistic benchmark. Experiments demonstrate potential improvements in fairness and the associated trade-offs with utility when applying bias mitigation strategies.
    @inproceedings{vladimirova2024fairjob, title={{FairJob}: A Real-World Dataset for Fairness in Online Systems}, author={Vladimirova, Mariia and Diemert, Eustache and Pavone, Federico}, booktitle={Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems, Datasets and Benchmarks Track}, year={2024} }

  • Bayesian Neural Network Unit Priors and Generalized Weibull-Tail Property
    Mariia Vladimirova, Julyan Arbel, Stéphane Girard
    ACMLAsian Conference on Machine Learning, 2021
    Scholar

  • Understanding Priors in Bayesian Neural Networks at the Unit Level
    Mariia Vladimirova, Jakob Verbeek, Pablo Mesejo, Julyan Arbel
    ICML2019
    Scholar
    We study the prior induced on deep Bayesian neural networks with Gaussian priors on weights, showing that output distributions become increasingly heavy-tailed with depth, following a Weibull-tail pattern. This provides theoretical grounding for prior design in practical BNNs and was among the first to formally characterize this depth-dependent behavior.
    @inproceedings{vladimirova2019understanding, title={Understanding Priors in {Bayesian} Neural Networks at the Unit Level}, author={Vladimirova, Mariia and Verbeek, Jakob and Mesejo, Pablo and Arbel, Julyan}, booktitle={International Conference on Machine Learning}, pages={6458--6467}, year={2019} }
